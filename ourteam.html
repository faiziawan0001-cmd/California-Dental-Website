About Dr. Arman Petrosyan

Dr. Petrosyan has over 20 years of experience and is committed to providing the absolute best in quality, thorough, and gentle dental care for every patient. He treats his patients like family and is exceptionally skilled in working with children.

Dr. Petrosyan finished his Bachelor of Science degree with high honors at the prestigious University of Southern California (USC). He was then accepted to USC’s top-rated dental school, earning his Doctor of Dental Surgery (DDS) degree and graduating in the top 10% of his class. During his school years, he took great satisfaction in participating in mission trips abroad to provide volunteer dental care for orphans in underprivileged areas of Mexico, Belize, and Armenia.

Professional Associations & Advanced Training

Dr. Petrosyan is a member in good standing with the American Dental Association (ADA), California Dental Association (CDA), and the San Fernando Valley Dental Society (SFVDS). He is well versed in aesthetic dentistry, from porcelain veneers and lumineers to cosmetic bonding, and has received training from world-renowned oral implantologists at the Nobel Biocare Implant Institute.

Personal Life

Known affectionately to his patients as "Dr. P.," he is fluent in English, Spanish, and Armenian. In his spare time, he enjoys quality time with his wife and two children, traveling, and exploring diverse culinary traditions.
