Welcome to California Dental

Your Dentist in San Fernando, CA

We understand that finding the right dentist for your family can feel overwhelming. Balancing busy schedules, navigating dental insurance, and managing dental anxiety are common challenges that shouldn't stand in the way of a healthy smile.

At California Dental, we are dedicated to changing that experience. Led by Dr. Arman Petrosyan, our team is committed to making high-quality, comprehensive dental care convenient, comfortable, and accessible for patients of all ages.
